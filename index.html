About TechTitan Portal

TechTitan Portal is an integrated web-based platform developed as a university group project to bring multiple useful digital tools and services together in one convenient system.

Designed with a focus on usability, accessibility, and modular architecture, the system provides a seamless user experience while leveraging modern web development practices.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide users with a convenient digital environment where multiple useful tools and services can be accessed through a single platform.
